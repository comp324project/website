About Us

Welcome to Appliqué: helping you build better resumes faster.

One of the most common tips for applying to jobs is to tailor your resume to the job description. Our goal is to make it easier to sort through skills and experiences relevant to a job description, cutting down on the time it takes to build new resumes for individual applications. We hope to reduce the stress applying to jobs can cause and give users an advantage on the job market.

Simply put, we make resumes easy!
